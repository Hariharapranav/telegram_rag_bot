Acme Corporation — Annual Leave & Time-Off Policy

1. Annual Paid Leave:
All full-time Acme Corporation employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2 days per completed calendar month of service.

2. Leave Rollover:
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the subsequent calendar year. Any carried-over days must be utilized on or before March 31st of the new calendar year, after which they expire without monetary compensation.

3. Sick Leave:
Acme Corporation provides 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A certified doctor's note is required for any continuous absence exceeding 2 business days.

4. Parental Leave:
Primary caregivers are granted 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Secondary caregivers (paternity leave) receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which may be taken consecutively or split across the child's first year.

5. Bereavement and Compassionate Leave:
Employees are eligible for up to 5 consecutive days of paid compassionate leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member.

6. Sabbatical Leave:
Employees with more than 3 consecutive years of service may apply for an unpaid sabbatical leave lasting between 1 and 3 months, subject to department head approval.
