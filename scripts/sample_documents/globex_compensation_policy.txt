Globex Corporation — Compensation, Bonus & Office Attendance Policy

1. Incentive Bonus Structure:
Globex employees are eligible for an annual performance bonus targeted at up to 30% of base salary, evaluated on company EBITDAR targets and individual quarterly objectives.

2. Equity & Stock Grants:
Eligible senior staff receive restricted stock units (RSUs) vesting on a four-year quarterly schedule with a standard one-year cliff.

3. On-Site Attendance Requirement:
Globex Corporation enforces mandatory full-time on-site work. Regular working hours are Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM. No regular hybrid or remote work arrangements are permitted.

4. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Zero Rollover:
Globex staff receive 18 days of unified PTO per year. Globex enforces a strict zero-rollover policy; any unused PTO is forfeited on December 31st without payout.
